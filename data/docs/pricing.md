# Orbit Pricing and Plans

Orbit is the project management product built by Lumen Labs. This page describes the
available plans, what each costs, and how billing works.

## Plans overview

| Plan       | Annual billing             | Monthly billing          | Users        | Storage |
|------------|----------------------------|--------------------------|--------------|---------|
| Free       | $0                         | $0                       | up to 3      | 1 GB    |
| Team       | $12 per user per month     | $15 per user per month   | up to 50     | 100 GB  |
| Business   | $24 per user per month     | $30 per user per month   | unlimited    | 1 TB    |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing             | Not available            | 100 seat minimum | Custom |

The Free plan is limited to 2 active projects. Team, Business and Enterprise include
unlimited projects.

## Plan features

- SAML single sign-on (SSO) is included on the Business and Enterprise plans.
- Enterprise customers get a dedicated customer success manager.
- Advanced reporting dashboards are available on Business and Enterprise.

## Annual billing discount

Paying annually saves 20% compared to monthly billing on the Team and Business plans.

## Free trial

New workspaces can start a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is
required to start the trial. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan
unless a paid plan is selected.

## Payment methods

Orbit accepts credit cards on all paid plans. ACH bank transfer is available for annual
plans only. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice.

## Nonprofit discount

Registered nonprofit organizations receive a 50% discount on the Team and Business plans.

## Price changes

Lumen Labs gives existing customers 60 days of notice before any price change takes effect.

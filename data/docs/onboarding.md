# Getting Started with Orbit

This guide walks new administrators through setting up an Orbit workspace and inviting
their team.

## Creating a workspace

Every Orbit account starts with a workspace. The workspace URL slug must be between 3 and
30 characters and may only contain lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

## Inviting members

Owners and Admins can invite members by email from Settings > Members. Invitation links
expire after 7 days; an expired invitation can be resent at any time.

## Roles

Orbit has four roles:

- **Owner** - full control, including billing and deleting the workspace.
- **Admin** - manages members, settings and integrations.
- **Member** - creates and edits projects and tasks.
- **Guest** - can only access the projects they are explicitly invited to.

Guests are free. Each paid seat allows up to 5 guests.

## Importing existing work

Orbit can import projects from Trello, Asana and Jira. You can also import tasks from a CSV
file; a single CSV import supports up to 10,000 rows.

## Templates

Orbit ships with more than 40 project templates, including sprint planning, content
calendars and product launches.

## Mobile apps

Orbit mobile apps are available for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere in Orbit to see all keyboard shortcuts. Press `Cmd+K` (macOS) or `Ctrl+K`
(Windows and Linux) to open the command palette.

# Orbit Support and Service Level Policy

This policy describes how Lumen Labs supports Orbit customers, the service level commitments
for each plan, and how refunds work.

## Support channels

Email support is available on all plans. Live chat is available on the Team, Business and
Enterprise plans. Phone support is available on the Enterprise plan only. Free plan
customers also have access to the community forum.

## Support hours

Standard support hours are 9:00 to 18:00 CET and 9:00 to 18:00 ET, Monday to Friday.
Enterprise customers receive 24/7 support for Severity 1 incidents.

## Response times

| Severity   | Description                       | First response target |
|------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Severity 1 | Orbit is down for all users       | 1 hour (Enterprise)   |
| Severity 2 | A core feature is unavailable     | 4 business hours      |
| Severity 3 | General question or minor issue   | 1 business day        |

Free plan requests are handled on a best-effort basis.

## Uptime SLA

Lumen Labs commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the Business and Enterprise plans. If monthly
uptime falls below 99.9%, affected customers receive a service credit of 10% of their monthly
fee; below 99.0%, the credit is 25%.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are non-refundable. Annual plans can be cancelled for a prorated refund within
the first 30 days of the billing period. To request a refund, the workspace Owner emails
billing@lumenlabs.example from the address on the account.
